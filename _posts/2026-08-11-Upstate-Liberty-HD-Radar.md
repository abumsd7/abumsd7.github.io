HD Radar Maps of Upstate Liberty.  
This is a combination of DimZet and AdusPL's renders.  
[An earlier version could be found inside the Upstate Liberty topic.](https://gtaforums.com/topic/881417-upstateliberty/?do=findComment&comment=1072729559)

<!--more-->

These maps were path traced, path corrected (to best of my ability) and colored via Inkscape.  
Not as detailed as what DimZet would make though as his were based on map renders, but still high quality thanks to paths.

### Contains:
* III classic colors.
* III classic but with roads and sea colors.
* LCS colors.
* IV colors.
