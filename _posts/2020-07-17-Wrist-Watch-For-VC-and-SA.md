Tommy wears a golden watch so why not use a golden watch to display time? It's better than using a phone because there were no modern phones in the 1980s.

<!--more-->

### Features:
* Displays Hours and minutes.
* SA version displays weekdays.
* 2 display modes: either on-screen all the time, or shown by holding `Q`.
* In SA version, CJ will perform a "Look at the watch" animation if `T` is pressed while holding `Q`.
* Easily customizable watch faces, needles, and day card (SA only) using MagicTXD and editing `watch.txd` in models folder (`watch_sa.txd` in SA).
* Scale and position customizable via `watch.ini` config file.

### Compatibility:
* Works with GTA VC 1.0 exe and GTA SA US 1.0 exe.
