A complete HUD modification which improves exponentially over the original IV HUD CLEO mod by ThirteenAG.

<!--more-->

### Features:
* **Customizability**: The components on the screen, their positions, scaling, and color can be easily modified through configuration files. Entire HUD is rebuilt to allow flexibility and real-time customization.
* **New Health/Armor System**: Similar to the system used in GTAIV Hud Mod by _AG. Allows painless customization with no need to recolor 400+ textures again.
* **Phone**: Imported from Rectangular HUD and now houses mission timers in addition to clock.
* **Animations**: Smooth fading/hiding animations for money and ammo, similar to GTA IV.
* **Wanted Stars & Radio**: GTA IV/EFLC style wanted stars and full GTA IV Radio HUD system.
* **Mode Presets**: Choose your favourite mode (IV / TLAD / TBOGT / Classic III/VC) and the color scheme and radar update accordingly.

### GTA IV Scrolling Radio (v1.7 & v1.8 Updates):
1. Configure icon size and gap.
2. Toggle between colorful and grey icons.
3. Quick mute radio by pressing `X`.
4. Bicycle display toggles and smooth fade-out animations.
