A modification for GTA III and GTA Vice City which adds rectangular radar, HUD, and menu elements based off the GTA V HUD!

<!--more-->

### Versions:
* **GTA Vice City**: Rectangular HUD v4.0 (ASI plugin, major upgrade)
* **GTA III**: Rectangular HUD v1.2 (ASI plugin, minor update)

### Features of GTA VC Rect-HUD (v4.0):
#### Menu:
* Revamped Menu layouts, completely customizable positioning for each screen.
* New Keyboard setup and Player Skin setup screens.
* Transparent Blur effects on Menu background and Weapon wheel toggle. Blur effect, colour, and saturation are customizable.
* New frontend textures — Game cover, original menu backgrounds, and mouse icons.
* All menu, HUD, and font textures relocated to `/rect_hud_charlesvercetti` folder. Mod works purely as plug-and-play.
* New `GXT_new.csv` which houses all GXT strings in `/rect_hud_charlesvercetti` folder.
* New Control bar at bottom right — replaces helper text and improves navigation.
* Skip Intro and Game Auto-loader toggles.

#### HUD:
* Improved Message box with message stacking.
* Simple GPS navigation with waypoint.
* Round entity and destination markers.
* Money difference indicator.
* Rewritten Weapon and Radio wheel logic with blur background and slow-motion activation.
* Improved crosshairs with hit-indicator.
* Auto-generated config files.
