Named after "SUMO" (the in-game parody of Sony Corporation).  
Play in-game radio stations and MP3 songs while on foot — no need to sit in a car.

<!--more-->

### What does it do:
* Plays custom MP3 stations based on folder names and content in `mp3_stations`. Just create new folders inside `mp3_stations` for new stations and fill them with songs.
* Plays in-game radio stations.
* [SA only] Displays in-game song, commercial, and ident track info.
* [SA only] Displays song names for all available in-game songs.
* [SA only] Randomizes in-game radio just like the base game.

### Other Features:
* Consists of era-specific parodied Walkman players as background images:
  * **Vice City**: Walkman WM-F28 (1986-87)
  * **San Andreas**: Walkman WM-B603 (1992)
  * **GTA 3**: Walkman WM-GX400 (2001)
* Config files for updating player and list positions and texture scales.
* Ability to either fade out the music player or keep it on screen while music is playing.
* Turns off in-game radio stations while in car.
* Text scroll from left to right.
* Compatible with v1.0 exes on all 3 games.

### Controls:
* **L**: Open / Close player
* **Up / Down**: Change MP3 tracks
* **Enter**: Select MP3 track / radio station
* **Left / Right**: Change stations
* **+ / -**: Adjust volume
