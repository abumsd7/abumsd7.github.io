A by-product of CharlesVercetti's GTA V HUD mod. Isolated and expanded for the legacy trilogy.  
Replaces GXT files with a merged CSV document for easy and live editing. Reads `.fxt` files in your CLEO folder.

<!--more-->

### Features:
- Reads the `.gxt` from your game folder (american, french, german, italian, spanish).
- Generates a merged `.csv` document containing all keys and values in `charlesvercetti_mods/EasyGXT` folder on first run.
- Hooks in-game `CText` to read from `.csv`.
- Allows editing of GXT values with text processors like Notepad (avoid MS Excel to keep formatting).
- Enables UTF-8 encoding in GTA SA (v1.2).

### Changelog:
- **v1.2 (July 26)**: Fixed a null key/value issue during cutscene to game transition in GTA SA Intro. Added encoding toggle (ANSI 1252/UTF8) to GTA SA.
- **v1.1 (July 15)**: Fixed a deallocation issue when exiting the game.
